webOS Email

The classic webOS mail app, working with Gmail again

A project of the webOS Archive · Last updated November 5, 2026

What is webOS Email?

webOS Email is the original mail app from HP/Palm webOS (com.palm.app.email), restored and kept running by the webOS Archive, a volunteer, non-profit preservation project. It runs on the webOS devices people still own and love: the Palm Pre, Pixi and Veer, and the HP TouchPad.

When these devices were new, signing in to Gmail meant typing your Google password into the phone. Google switched that off in 2022, which left the built-in Gmail account unable to sign in at all. webOS Email brings it back using Google’s current, secure sign-in.

What it does

  • Loads your Gmail inbox and labels as folders, with the familiar webOS card-based mail interface.
  • Downloads message headers, bodies and attachments to your own device so you can read mail, including offline.
  • Composes, replies to and forwards mail, and sends it through your own Gmail account.
  • Marks messages read or unread, flags them, moves them between folders, and deletes them. Changes sync back to Gmail.
  • Runs in the background so new mail and notifications arrive without opening the app.

How it sends and receives Gmail

webOS Email is an ordinary mail client. It talks to Gmail directly over the standard mail protocols, and never uses a middleman server to read or relay your mail.

DirectionHow
ReceivingIMAP to imap.gmail.com, with the connection secured by TLS.
SendingSMTP to smtp.gmail.com, with the connection secured by TLS. Gmail keeps a copy in your Sent folder.
Signing inOAuth 2.0 sign-in with Google (XOAUTH2). The app never sees or stores your Google password.

Why the app asks for access to your Google data

webOS Email asks for permission to read, compose, send, and permanently delete all your email from Gmail (the https://mail.google.com/ scope). A mail client needs exactly this: to download your messages, send new ones, and to let you move or delete them. The IMAP and SMTP protocols can only be authorized with this scope, so a narrower one would not work.

Google dataUsed for
Your email address and profile nameIdentifying the account on the device and filling in the “From” address of messages you send.
Your messages, labels and attachmentsShowing them to you in the app and syncing your changes back.
Permission to send mailSending the messages you write, from your address.

What we do not do with it

  • Your mail is downloaded directly to your device. It is not copied to, stored on, or read by webOS Archive servers.
  • The sign-in helper handles only the OAuth sign-in codes and tokens needed to connect your device. It never handles your mail content.
  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or profiling, or share it with anyone.
  • No person at the webOS Archive reads your email.
  • webOS Email’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
The full details of what we collect and how we protect it are in our Privacy Policy. Use of the app is also covered by our Terms of Service.
