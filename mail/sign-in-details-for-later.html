Signing in, step by step

webOS devices are too old to display Google’s sign-in page themselves, so the webOS Archive runs a small sign-in helper that handles that one step in a modern browser:

  1. On your webOS device, add a Gmail account in Email. The device shows a short, one-time code and the address of the helper page.
  2. On a phone or computer, open the helper page and enter the code.
  3. You are taken to Google’s own sign-in and consent screen, where you choose your account and review exactly what you are allowing.
  4. Once you approve, the helper passes an access token to your device, which finds it by polling with the code. The code is single-use and expires quickly.
  5. Your device connects to Gmail directly and begins syncing. When the token expires, the helper refreshes it so you stay signed in.

You can end the app’s access at any time by removing the account from your device, or by visiting your Google Account’s third-party access page and removing webOS Email.
