Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information webOS Archive (“we”, “us”) collects when you use webosarchive.org and its services, including Email for webOS (www.webosarchive.org/mail), the webOS App Museum and App Catalog (appcatalog.webosarchive.org), the on-device Museum app, webOS Account, webOS Chat, and cloud app storage (together, the “Services”). We are a volunteer, non-profit preservation project and collect as little as we need to run the Services.

Information we collect

  • Server logs. Our web servers record your IP address, request time, requested URL, and browser or device user agent. These are used for security, abuse prevention, rate limiting, and troubleshooting.
  • Download and update-check logs. When you download an app or a device checks for updates, we record the app, time, IP address, the type of client (for example web or on-device app), and for devices a device identifier. We use this to produce aggregate statistics such as most-downloaded apps.
  • Account information. If you create an account (a webOS Account on a device, or an account for the web, PWA, or admin tools), we store your username, email address, a salted and hashed password, display name, device names, and authentication tokens. If you use webOS Chat, we also store a generated chat pass phrase.
  • Cloud app storage. Apps you use may store data against your account. This data is stored as opaque blobs scrambled by the app on your device; we do not read it and it is not used for any other purpose.
  • User map (optional). If you opt in, we estimate your approximate location from the IP address of your device’s most recent update check. We publish only a named place (the nearest large city, or your state or country, according to the precision you choose) together with your username and the date last seen. We never publish IP addresses or precise coordinates. You can opt out at any time from your account page.
  • Email you send us. If you contact us, we keep your message and address to respond.

How we use information

  • To operate, secure, and maintain the Services.
  • To authenticate you and sync your account data.
  • To compile aggregate usage statistics and reports.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, and to enforce rate limits.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Google user data (Email for webOS)

Email for webOS is a mail app for webOS devices that lets you read and send Gmail. If you choose to sign in with Google, this section describes how we handle the data we receive from Google.

  • What the app accesses. With your permission (the https://mail.google.com/ scope), the app accesses your Google account email address and profile name, and your Gmail messages, labels, and attachments, so it can display your mail, sync changes you make (such as reading, moving, or deleting messages), and send messages you write.
  • Where your mail goes. The app connects from your device directly to Gmail (IMAP and SMTP over TLS). Your mail is stored on your device only. It is not copied to, stored on, or read by webOS Archive servers.
  • What our sign-in helper stores. Because webOS devices cannot complete Google sign-in themselves, a webOS Archive sign-in helper performs the OAuth step in your browser. It handles only short-lived one-time codes and the OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to connect your device, which are held to hand them to your device and to refresh your access. It never handles the content of your mail. Tokens are deleted when they are delivered, expire, or are revoked.
  • How we use it. Only to provide and improve the user-facing mail features described above. We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. We do not sell it or transfer it to third parties, except as necessary to provide the app, to comply with law, or with your consent. No person at webOS Archive reads your mail.
  • Limited Use. webOS Archive’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access and deletion. You can remove the account from your device at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have any sign-in data we hold deleted, email us.

Sharing

We share information only when necessary: with service providers who host or deliver the Services (for example hosting, content delivery, and IP geolocation or place-lookup services used for the optional user map); when required by law; or to protect the rights, safety, and security of the Services and our users. Aggregate, non-identifying statistics may be published. The public archive export of app records contains no user data.

Cookies and local storage

We use only what is needed to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences (such as session cookies or browser local storage). We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Retention

Server and usage logs are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. Account data and app storage are kept until you delete them or your account is deleted. You can download or delete your app storage data from your account page.

Security

We use reasonable measures such as hashed passwords, token-based authentication, and HTTPS where supported. Older webOS devices that have not received the community update may only be able to connect over unencrypted HTTP, which offers less protection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your account information from your account page, or by emailing us.
  • Opt out of the user map at any time.
  • Download or delete your cloud app storage data.
  • Request deletion of your account by emailing us.

Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

International users

The Services are operated from the United States. By using them you understand your information may be processed in the United States and other countries.

Third-party links

The Services may link to third-party sites, such as Discord, GitHub, and archive.org. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it last changed. Continued use of the Services means you accept the updated policy.
